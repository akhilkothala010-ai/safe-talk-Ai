Model Evaluation

Understanding the performance, limitations, and fairness of our AI model.

Dataset & Models

Our project explored multiple architectures using the Jigsaw Toxic Comment Classification Challenge Dataset. While we trained our own baseline TF-IDF Logistic Regression and fine-tuned DistilBERT models for research, the live application uses a highly optimized production model.

  • Dataset Source: Jigsaw Toxic Comment Dataset (via Kaggle)
  • Research Models: Custom fine-tuned DistilBERT and TF-IDF Baseline.
  • Deployed Production Model: unitary/toxic-bert (via Hugging Face)

Fairness & Limitations

We actively run Fairness and Bias tests against our models. A naive toxicity detector can unfairly flag certain communities, identity terms, or colloquial slang. We measure false positives on harmless uses of terms such as:

  • Identity terms: e.g., references to race, gender, or sexual orientation.
  • Slang & AAVE: Harmless phrases (e.g., "That outfit is sick").
  • Sarcasm: Context-heavy statements.
Known Limitation: The AI model cannot fully understand context. Sarcastic comments or reclaimed slang within specific communities might be incorrectly flagged as toxic. We provide our predictions as suggestions, not absolute truths. Users are encouraged to provide feedback on incorrect predictions.
